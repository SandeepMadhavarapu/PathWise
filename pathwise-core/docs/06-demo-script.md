# 06 — The Two-Minute Demo Script

Write/refine this on day 1, BEFORE any code. Everything we build exists to make this script true.
Anything not in it, we do not build. **The video is half the score surface** — judges watch it (and
read the 500 words) before they ever open the link.

## Why NOT the obvious structure
The obvious script runs sequentially: F-1 stuff, then a residency screen, then an aid screen. That
reads as three tools taped together and buries the thesis. Instead we structure around **one fact
and one event**, so the cross-domain interaction is on screen the entire time.

## The script (target 1:55, hard cap 2:00)

**[0:00–0:12] The problem, one sentence.**
"International students deal with three offices — immigration, tuition residency, and financial aid.
None of them can see the whole student. So students get blindsided by problems no single office
would ever catch. This is Standing." (On screen: landing line + "View example student.")

**[0:12–0:35] One fact, two doors.**
Click Priya. Home screen. "Priya is on an F-1 visa. Watch what that one fact does." Highlight the
hero finding: F-1 status blocks Virginia in-state residency AND Virginia state aid — with the SCHEV
citation inline. "One fact. Two doors closed. And here's the exact regulation, on screen, not buried
in a disclaimer."

**[0:35–1:05] The ledger — proof it's real, not a chatbot.**
Open the timeline. The CPT ledger bar sits in the amber zone: "Priya has 342 days of full-time CPT
across two schools and two degree levels. The cliff is 365 — cross it and she loses OPT entirely.
54 of those days come from two *part-time* internships that overlapped and aggregated to full-time —
something she'd never see herself. The bachelor's CPT is partitioned out because the cap resets by
level. A chatbot can't do this arithmetic. A rules engine can." (Show the stacked bars.)

**[1:05–1:40] The money moment — one event, four consequences.**
"Now Priya does something good — she signs a job offer." Click "I got a job." The four-consequence
screen appears. Read the two counter-intuitive ones: "Her unemployment counter did NOT stop — her
start date is in the future, and those days still count. And a new 10-day SEVIS reporting deadline
just appeared. She did the right thing and the system caught two hidden risks no single office
tracks." Point at the stale-finding flag: "and an earlier finding just recomputed."

**[1:40–1:55] The scale line + close.**
"Every rule is a cited JSON file. Fifty states are in the app; adding one is authoring a file, not
writing code. Standing doesn't answer one question — it holds a student's whole history and notices
the things that fall between the offices." (Show the rule-pack viewer + coverage map for 3 seconds.)

## Recording discipline
- Record 5+ takes. No dead air, no "let me just click here."
- Priya's story must land in the first 15 seconds.
- Give the money moment (1:05–1:40) room to breathe — it's the beat that wins.
- Feature-freeze first (Aug 16), THEN record (Aug 16–17).
