## The script

**[0:00–0:12] The problem — one sentence, on the landing page.**
> "An international student's future is decided by three offices that never talk to each other —
> immigration, tuition residency, and financial aid. Each sees a fragment. None sees the student.
> This is PathWise."

*On screen: the landing line, then click **View example student**.*

---

**[0:12–0:32] One fact, two doors. (The thesis.)**
> "Meet Priya. She's on an F-1 visa. Watch what that one fact does. Virginia's own guidelines say a
> student-visa holder cannot establish domicile — so in-state tuition is closed. And the same
> status independently blocks Virginia state aid. One fact. Two doors, in two different buildings.
> And the regulation is right there on screen, not buried in a disclaimer."

*On screen: the hero finding + the two red door cards with their SCHEV citations.*

---

**[0:32–0:47] It remembers. (My Journey.)**
> "PathWise doesn't start from zero every time. It holds her whole history — two institutions, six
> events, nothing to re-explain."

*Click **View Priya's full journey** → scroll the timeline → click one CPT row so it expands and
shows the evidence and why it matters. Point at the gray row:*
> "And where the record is genuinely incomplete, it says so, instead of guessing."

---

**[0:47–1:07] The arithmetic a chatbot can't do. (CPT ledger.)**
*Back to the dashboard, ledger bar on screen.*
> "342 full-time CPT days across two schools and two degree levels. The cliff is 365 — cross it and
> she loses OPT entirely. 54 of those days came from two *part-time* internships that overlapped
> and aggregated to full-time. She would never have seen them. Her bachelor's days are partitioned
> out, because the cap resets by level. That's a rules engine, not a guess."

---

**[1:07–1:22] It shows its work. (Finding detail.)**
*Click **See full reasoning**.*
> "Every answer opens up: the claim, the exact regulation quoted, the date we verified it, and the
> office that actually decides. PathWise advises — the office decides."

---

**[1:22–1:45] It changes its mind. (What Changed? — the money moment.)**
*Go to `/student/changed`.*
> "Here's the part I'd watch. One document is missing — the level change between her two schools.
> With it, the engine reads 342 days and her OPT survives. Without it, the same engine over the same
> authorizations reads 552 — past the cliff, OPT gone. Two honest readings, opposite sides of the
> line. So PathWise says 'unable to verify' instead of picking one."

*Click **Add the missing document**.*
> "Add the document, and it recomputes live. That's a system that reasons — and admits what it
> doesn't know."

---

**[1:45–2:00] Scale + close.**
> "Every rule is a cited JSON file. Fifty states are in the app; adding one is authoring a file, not
> writing code. No account, nothing stored on a server. Three offices decide your fate. None of them
> can see the whole you. PathWise does."

## Recording discipline
- **Feature freeze first**, then record. Bugs only after freeze.
- Do **5+ takes**. No dead air, no "let me just click here," no mouse hunting.
- Pre-open the tabs / warm the pages so nothing loads slowly on camera.
- Priya's stakes must land in the first 15 seconds.
- Give **1:22–1:45** room to breathe — it's the beat that wins.
- Clean screen capture at 1280px+, no browser clutter, no personal bookmarks visible.
- Upload early to YouTube/Vimeo/Loom — processing takes hours. Set to public or unlisted, and
  **test the link in an incognito window.**

## Fallback if a take runs long
Cut in this order: (1) shorten the scale line at 1:45, (2) trim the journey beat to 10s,
(3) trim finding-detail to one sentence. **Never cut the "What Changed?" beat** — that's the win.
