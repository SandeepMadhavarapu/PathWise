# 13 — The 2-Minute Lightning Demo

For a lightning round, a booth walk-up, or a judge who says "you have two minutes."
Labels copied from the shipped build, same rule as `12-final-demo-script.md`: **if a label here does
not match the screen, the script is wrong, not the app.**

This is **not** the 5-minute script compressed. It is a different script with one job: get a judge
to the refusal and out again, with the honesty claim planted on the way in.

**Click path — four clicks, no typing:**
`/` → **See the worked example →** → **One missing document decides this count** →
**Use a sample document →** → tick attestation → **Add to my record** → stop.

**What you give up, on purpose:** the finding detail, the rule-pack viewer, `/coverage`, `/check`,
`/moment`. Do not try to fit them. A judge who wants them will ask.

---

## [0:00–0:25] Landing — problem, honesty, person

*Landing already on screen. Do not scroll yet.*

> "An international student's future is decided by three offices that never speak to each other.
> Immigration, tuition residency, financial aid. None of them sees the whole student."

*Point at **THE RULES ARE DATA** — one gesture, no pause.*

> "One state modelled in full, two partially, forty-three where we captured the source and stopped,
> five we could not verify. Those numbers are read out of the rule files — I can't inflate them by
> editing the map."

*Scroll once, to the three cards.*

> "This is Priya. Master's student, two institutions, twenty-three days from a limit that would end
> her OPT eligibility. One fact — she holds F-1 status. Immigration: 23 days of margin. Virginia
> residency: blocked. Virginia state aid: blocked. All three computed on this page, right now, each
> carrying the regulation and the office that decides it."

---

## [0:25–0:45] The ledger — the computation

*Click **See the worked example →**, scroll to **The computation a chatbot can't do**.*

> "342 full-time CPT days across two schools. The cliff is 365 — cross it and OPT at this level is
> gone. Fifty-four of those days came from two *part-time* internships that overlapped and
> aggregated to full-time. Each authorization looks compliant on its own. That is why this needs a
> ledger and not a chat window."

---

## [0:45–1:45] The refusal — the whole demo ⭐

*Click **One missing document decides this count**.*

> "One document is missing from her file — the form recording her level change between the two
> schools. With it the engine reads 342 days. Without it, the same engine over the same
> authorizations reads 552."

*Trace the span across the red **365-DAY CLIFF** line.*

> "The cliff sits between them. Two honest readings, opposite sides of the line. One keeps her work
> authorization after she graduates; the other ends it. So PathWise says **unable to verify** — and
> stops."

*Click **Use a sample document →**.*

> "Read in this tab. 542 bytes. SHA-256. Never uploaded."

*Tick the attestation. Click **Add to my record**.*

> **[SILENCE. THREE SECONDS. Watch the span collapse.]**

> "One reading is ruled out. The count is determinate. And it still doesn't overclaim: it read the
> file's bytes, never its words — so the level change is **asserted**, not extracted. Her word, with
> a document attached."

---

## [1:45–2:00] Close

> "Three offices decide an international student's future. None can see the whole student. PathWise
> sees all three, shows the regulation behind every answer, names the office that actually decides —
> and when the record genuinely can't settle a question, it says so instead of guessing.
>
> That last part is the hard one."

---

## Timing discipline

| Beat | Budget | If you're over |
|---|---|---|
| Landing | 0:25 | Drop the four counts, keep Priya |
| Ledger | 0:20 | Drop the "each looks compliant" sentence |
| **Refusal** | **1:00** | **Never cut. Take the time from the landing.** |
| Close | 0:15 | Cut to the last two sentences |

**The three-second silence after Add to my record is scripted.** It is the single most memorable
beat you have, and every instinct in a timed round will tell you to fill it. Don't.

## The one-sentence version, if you get ten seconds

> "It's a reasoning engine over one student record that answers immigration, residency and aid
> together — and refuses to answer when the record genuinely can't settle it."
